In the multiplayer game there will be 2 teams. Each player gets to be either part of the submarine team or the ships team. The Leviathan server will be responsible for matching up the players and assign them either submarine or ship team. The sub team will have an underwater view of what is happening and the ship team will have a surface view of what is happening. The ship team is planning to land a large continget of forces on the beaches of the sub teams nation and the sub team is trying to thwart this attempt. The sub team is waiting stealthly for the ship teams arrival, both teams have the capbailiyt to detect each other using passive or active sonars. When the ship team detects a torpedo it can deploy anit-torpedo-torpedos and also deploy anit sub torpedos and depth charges. The torpedos on both sides can be of the homing type so that its path is difficult to predict. The Leviathan server is responsible to update the game state in real time for both the parties so that they can both see the deployed torpedos etc. In the future each team may have multiple players.
